When the voice cannot be made or used: three endings

Shown on the Create page in place of the progress card, and on the video's detail page. Each says what happened, what was or was not charged, and what to do next. Amounts use 10 credits per voice. A voice that failed or never arrived cannot be reused.

1 · The voice could not be made (or came out empty)

We could not make your voice

What happenedSomething went wrong while making the voice from your script.
Your creditsThe 10 credits for the voice were returned to your wallet.
What you can doStart over. Making a new voice costs 10 credits again.

2 · We cannot tell if the voice was made

We could not confirm your voice

What happenedSomething unexpected happened while making your voice, and no voice reached you.
Your creditsThe 10 credits for the voice were returned to your wallet (D-048). Our team has been told and will check this job.
What you can doStart over. Making a new voice costs 10 credits again.

3 · The voice is too long for a video

Your voice is longer than a video can be

What happenedThe voice we made from your script is longer than the longest video we can make right now.
Your creditsThe 10 credits for this voice were kept, because the voice was made. We saved the voice for you (D-040).
What you can doMake a new video with this voice. No new voice is made and no voice credits are charged. You will listen and approve the price again.
PLACEHOLDER (D-044): offered only once the video model's maximum length is checked and recorded
