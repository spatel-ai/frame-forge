After payment: the Buy credits dialog's four endings

Shown inside the same 480 px dialog after Razorpay Checkout closes. Credits are added only when our server confirms the payment; the browser's success is never enough.

1 · Confirmed by the server

Payment received

500 credits were added to your wallet.

You paid
₹500
Balance now
1,000 credits
2 · Still confirming (checked every 2 s)

Confirming your payment

This usually takes a few seconds. Please keep this window open.

After one minute, the text changes to: "This is taking longer than usual. If money left your account, the credits will appear in your wallet within 30 minutes. You can close this window."
3 · Payment failed

The payment didn't go through

No credits were added. Try again, or choose another way to pay in the Razorpay window.

4 · Window closed before paying

Payment cancelled

You closed the payment window, so no credits were added. If you did finish paying, the credits will appear in your wallet within 30 minutes.
