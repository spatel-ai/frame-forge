When the video cannot be made: three endings, and the testing limit

Shown on the Create page in place of the progress card, and on the video's detail page. Example amounts: a 12-second video costs 240 credits, plus 10 credits for the voice. A failed job keeps its voice (D-043).

1 · We could not start the video

We could not start your video

What happenedWe were not able to send your photo and voice to be made into a video.
Your creditsNothing was taken for the video: the 240 credits on hold were released. The 10 credits for the voice stay paid.
What you can doListen and approve again to retry. Your voice is still here.
Go to Videos
2 · The video failed or took too long

We could not make your video

What happenedThe video could not be finished, or it took too long, so we stopped.
Your creditsThe 240 credits on hold were returned to your wallet. The 10 credits for the voice were not returned, because the voice was made.
What you can doYour voice is saved. Make a new video with this voice: no new voice is made and no voice credits are charged. You will listen and approve the price again (240 credits for this 12-second voice).
Start over
3 · We cannot tell if the video started

We could not confirm your video

What happenedYour request may have reached the video maker, but we did not get an answer, so we cannot tell whether your video was made.
Your creditsThe 240 credits for the video were taken, because the video may have been made. With the 10 for the voice, that is 250 credits. Our team has been told and will check this video.
What you can doYour voice is saved. Make a new video with this voice: no new voice is made and no voice credits are charged. You will listen and approve the price again (240 credits for this 12-second voice).

4 · Daily limit of 50 videos (development and testing only)

You've reached today's limit of 50 videos

What happenedThis limit exists only while we build and test. It is not applied in production, and there is never a limit on voices (D-046). It shows on the approve step.
Your creditsNothing was held. Nothing was charged for the video.
What you can doTry again tomorrow, or ask us to raise the limit while testing.
Development and testing only
